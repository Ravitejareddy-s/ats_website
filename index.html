About Us

More than illumination.

At A2S Technical Services, we believe lighting is more than illumination : it is an essential element that defines architecture, elevates spaces, and creates memorable experiences.

Our team delivers world-class lighting solutions that blend creativity, engineering precision, and energy efficiency across architectural, decorative, facade, landscape, and industrial applications.

From concept to completion, we bring your vision to light.

Mission

To engineer and deliver intelligent lighting and immersive technology solutions that elevate spaces, strengthen brands, and create meaningful experiences through innovation, precision, and reliable execution.

Vision

To be a trusted leader in integrated lighting and immersive technology, shaping the future of experiential spaces through creativity, engineering excellence, advanced technology, and uncompromising quality.

What We Do

Lighting, end‑to‑end.

Design, supply and technical delivery across every discipline of the built environment.

Lighting Design & Supply

Concept-to-delivery lighting schemes with the fixtures, drivers and controls to match.

Architectural Lighting

Integrated light that defines form, structure and atmosphere across interiors and exteriors.

Decorative Lighting

Statement fixtures and feature pieces that bring character, warmth and craft to a space.

Facade & Landscape

Dynamic facade media and landscape lighting that make buildings and grounds come alive at night.

Industrial & Sports

High-performance, energy-efficient lighting engineered for large-scale and demanding venues.
